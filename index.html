05 · Our Story

A full table is our love language.

Wideplate started with a simple habit of home: when guests arrive in San Antonio, you feed them well. We're a women-owned kitchen with deep Zambales roots, serving the classics we grew up on: cooked slow, seasoned with confidence, and served on plates that live up to the name.

"When guests arrive, you feed them well."
photo: owners behind wideplate, candid

Come before the boat to Anawangin, or after a long day on the sand at Pundaquit. Either way, arrive hungry.
